I would like to explain four projects from my experience.

Project 1 — AI Claims Intelligence Platform
The first project was an AI Claims Intelligence Platform.
The main goal was to reduce manual work in insurance claim processing.
Claims data came from both databases and documents.
We used GPT-4o and NLP to read the documents and extract useful information like claim amount, policy details, incident date, and customer details.
After that, we prepared the data and created useful features for the model.
We used XGBoost for classification and risk-related prediction.
We also compared information from different documents to find differences in dates, amounts, or policy details.
The main result was less manual work and better claim analysis.

Project 2 — LLM Text Analytics and Customer Feedback
The second project was related to customer feedback analysis.
The goal was to understand a large amount of customer feedback without reading everything manually.
We used LLMs and NLP for sentiment analysis, theme detection, and finding repeated customer issues.
For example, if many customers were complaining about payment problems, the system could identify that as a common issue.
We checked the model results using sample data and metrics like precision, recall, and F1 score.
The main result was faster customer feedback analysis and easier issue tracking.

Project 3 — MLOps and Model Deployment
The third project was related to MLOps.
The goal was to manage machine learning models after they were built.
I worked on areas like model versioning, prediction logging, deployment support, monitoring, and retraining.
We also checked for data drift, which means the new data starts becoming different from the data used to train the model.
If model performance went down, or new data was available, the model could be retrained.
This project helped me understand what happens to a model after development and how it is managed in production.

Project 4 — Product and Service Recommendation System
The fourth project was a recommendation system.
The goal was to show users products or services based on their interests and past behaviour.
We worked with two main methods: collaborative filtering and content-based filtering.
Collaborative filtering means recommending something based on what similar users liked.
Content-based filtering means recommending something similar to what the user already liked or used.
We used past purchase and usage data to create recommendations.
The main goal was to make the recommendations more useful for each customer.

Your overall work process
In most data science projects, my process is simple.
First, I understand the business problem.
Then I collect and study the data.
After that, I clean the data and check for missing values, wrong values, duplicates, and unusual data.
Then I create useful features and prepare the data for the model.
After that, I train the model and test it using the right metrics.
If the model performs well, the next step is deployment and monitoring.
I also believe that not every problem needs machine learning. Sometimes simple SQL analysis or reporting can solve the problem.
So I try to use the right solution based on the problem.
